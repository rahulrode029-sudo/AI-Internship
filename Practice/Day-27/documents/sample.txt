Company AI Policy

Artificial Intelligence can be used to improve productivity,
customer support, document analysis, and business decision making.

Employees must protect confidential company information
when using AI systems.

Sensitive customer information must not be entered into
public AI systems without proper authorization.

AI-generated results should be reviewed by employees before
important business decisions are made.

The company encourages responsible AI development,
human oversight, data privacy, security, and transparency.

Employees working on AI projects should follow the approved
development process and use Git for source code collaboration.
