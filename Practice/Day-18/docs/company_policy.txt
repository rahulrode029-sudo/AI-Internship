About XYZ Company

XYZ Company is a mid-sized technology firm founded in 2010,
 headquartered in Pune, India. It specializes in providing cloud-based 
 software solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). 
 The company started as a two-person startup and has grown to over 500 employees 
 across three offices in India and one in Singapore.

XYZ Company's flagship product, XYZCloud, is an all-in-one business 
management platform that includes modules for inventory management, 
invoicing, payroll, and customer relationship management (CRM). 
The platform currently serves more than 12,000 active business clients across 15 countries.

In 2018, XYZ Company raised $8 million in Series A funding led by Nexus Venture Partners,
followed by a $25 million Series B round in 2021. The company reported annual revenue of 
$40 million in the fiscal year 2023-2024, growing at a year-over-year rate of 22%.

The leadership team is headed by CEO Ananya Sharma, who co-founded the company along with CTO Rajesh Verma.
The company follows a hybrid work model and has been recognized as one of the "Top 50 Startups to Watch"
by a leading business magazine in 2022.

XYZ Company is currently investing heavily in AI-driven features, including automated invoice processing and
predictive inventory alerts, aiming to launch these features by Q2 2026. The company's mission statement emphasizes
making enterprise-grade software accessible and affordable for smaller businesses.
